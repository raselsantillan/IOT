Introduction

The Concept

"Imagine if every object in your house communicates like a person... not with words, but with data." IoT gives physical objects a digital voice.

The Problem

  • Fires spread quickly and silently.
  • Traditional alarms are often local-only (no remote alert).
  • Delayed response leads to devastating damage.

The Solution

An integrated IoT smoke detection system that continuously monitors for fire hazards, automatically triggers safety responses, and sends instant alerts to keep you protected 24/7.

Project Objectives

Main Goal

To improve safety in homes and offices using smart sensors that detect early fire signs and alert users immediately, preventing small incidents from becoming emergencies.

1

Early Detection

Monitor smoke, gas (MQ-2), and light intensity (LDR) to detect hazards instantly.

2

Smart Alerts

Send automatic notifications to smartphones via Cloud, even when users are off-site.

3

Auto Response

Trigger local alarms (Buzzer) and simulate safety measures (Water Valve Servo).
